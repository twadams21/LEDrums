claim: `initialProject()` is executed at module scope (main.ts:155) and, when `default.local.json` exists, unconditionally calls `loadProject(LIVE_PROJECT)` — which does `JSON.parse` + `parseProject` (throws ZodError) + `assertProjectIntegrity` (throws ReferentialIntegrityError) with no try/catch anywhere in the chain.
