claim: `PixelOutput.send(universe: number, ...)` (interfaces.ts:6) is the single seam behind which BOTH Art-Net and sACN sit, but the two protocols have different validity domains for that number: Art-Net universe is a 15-bit field where 0 is legal (artnet.ts:23-24 masks to `& 0x7f` on the MSB), while E1.31 universes run 1..63999 and 0 is reserved. The DMX map is 0-based — dmx-map.ts:102 seeds the cursor at 0 unless an output declares `startUniverse`, and dmx-map.test.ts:87 pins `[0, 1]` as the expected output. When `settings.protocol === 'sacn'` (output-manager.ts:54) that same 0 flows straight into `encodeE131(universe & 0xffff, …)` (sacn.ts:49) and into `sacnMulticastAddress(0)` → `"239.255.0.0"` (sacn.ts:14), which is not a valid sACN universe group. No conversion, clamp, or validation exists at the seam or in either adapter. The invariants that should live on a `Universe` value object — protocol-specific range, and the base-offset relationship between Art-Net and sACN numbering — are enforced nowhere and depend on the operator manually setting `startUniverse: 1` on every output.
