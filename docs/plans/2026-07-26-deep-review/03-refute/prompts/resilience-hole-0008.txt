claim: `createHttpTransport` collapses every non-2xx into one opaque `Error('ingest responded ' + status)`, and `doShip` catches that identically regardless of status: increment `failures`, log locally, retain the entire batch, back off. There is no notion of a permanent failure. The Worker returns 401 on a rotated `TELEMETRY_TOKEN`, 400 on a schema-drifted batch, and 413 when a body exceeds `MAX_BODY_BYTES`/`MAX_BACKUP_BODY_BYTES` — all of which will fail identically on every subsequent attempt, forever, at the 30-minute backoff ceiling. Because `doShip` always ships the WHOLE store as one batch, a single poison item blocks every later item behind it; the queue only 'recovers' by silently discarding oldest-first once `maxItems`/`maxBytes` is exceeded, which for the backups queue (maxItems 100, maxBytes 8MB, append-only keying) means the drummer's off-site snapshots quietly stop existing. And the only signal is `log()`, which defaults to `console.error` — there is no queue-health surface in the UI and no monitor event, by explicit design ('the queue reports its own failures only through the injected log').
