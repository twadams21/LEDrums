Gesture → model
The box is aligned to the drum's own axis, not the screen.
L handles (along the spindle) → depth, the hoop-stack length.
W handles (across it) → diameter.
Corners → both. Rotate in a plate turns the drum about that plate's normal axis (Top→Z, Front→Y, Left/Right→X), composed onto its existing rotation.
When a drum's axis points at you, depth is not editable in that plate and its L handles are hidden.
